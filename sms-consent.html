SMS Opt-In & Consent

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Malachim Messenger (a sole proprietorship of Benjamin Brewer, Bethesda, Maryland) operates ClinicFlow AI, an AI voice receptionist that answers phone calls for medical practices and other businesses. This page explains how people agree to receive text messages from us, what those messages contain, and how to stop them.

Program name

Malachim Messenger / ClinicFlow AI call-summary text messages.

How callers opt in

Callers opt in verbally during a phone call with our AI receptionist. Near the end of the call, after taking the caller's message, the assistant asks for permission before any text is sent. The assistant reads this disclosure:

“Would you like a text message from Malachim Messenger confirming your message to the office? Message frequency varies, and message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help. Do you agree to receive this text?”

A text is sent only if the caller clearly answers yes and confirms the mobile number to text. If the caller says no, or does not answer, no text message is sent. The caller's answer is saved with the call recording and transcript as a record of consent.

Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase or of receiving care. Callers who decline still have their message delivered to the office.

Staff alert messages

Businesses that use ClinicFlow AI may choose to receive text alerts about new calls. Each staff member's mobile number is added only at that business's request, and each staff member gives consent to receive these alerts. Staff can reply STOP at any time to stop alerts.

Messages you will receive

  • A confirmation that your message was received, with a summary of what you told us.
  • A secure link to review and correct your call summary, when the office offers it.
  • Follow-up replies about the call you made.

We do not send marketing or promotional texts through this program.

Sample messages

Malachim Messenger: Thanks for calling. Your message to the office was received and staff will call you back within 1 business day. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. Msg & data rates may apply.

Malachim Messenger: We received your call and will return it at this number. If this is an emergency, call 911. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.

Frequency and cost

Message frequency varies, usually 1–3 messages per call. Message and data rates may apply.

How to opt out or get help

  • Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. You will receive one confirmation message and no further texts unless you opt in again.
  • Reply HELP for help, or email benjamin@malachim-messenger.com.

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. See our Privacy Policy and SMS Terms.
