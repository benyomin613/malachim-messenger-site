For outpatient clinics

ClinicFlow AI

A voice AI receptionist built for medical practices. It answers patient calls, gathers the details staff need, and produces a message summary that can be edited and exported to Word for the EMR.

  • Answers calls 24/7 — no voicemail backlog
  • Captures name, date of birth, callback number, email, and reason for call
  • Directs callers with emergency symptoms to emergency care
  • Includes a calculator for revenue lost to missed calls

Clinical tool

HbA1c Prediction Calculator

A free web calculator that estimates how HbA1c may change when diabetes medications or supplements are started, continued, or stopped — built on published clinical formulas.

  • Converts HbA1c to estimated average glucose (ADAG formula)
  • Estimates HbA1c from 7-point fingerstick readings or fasting glucose
  • Covers major diabetes drug classes and common supplements
  • For education only — not a substitute for medical advice

About

“Malachim” is Hebrew for messengers — and that is what we build.

Malachim Messenger was founded in 2026 by Benjamin Brewer, MD, MA, a physician-developer who combines clinical experience with modern software engineering.

Having worked a busy front desk — answering dozens of calls a day while rooming patients — he saw firsthand how many calls go unanswered and how much gets lost between the phone and the chart. Malachim Messenger exists to close that gap with AI assistants that are accurate, safe, and easy for staff to trust.

Every product is physician-created, and we strive to provide superlative service to every practice and business we work with.

Based in Bethesda, Maryland.
